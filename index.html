Lindsay Hartley

About

Director

Most directors come to the job from one discipline. Lindsay Hartley came from all of them.

With more than thirty features for Lifetime, Hulu, Amazon, and Tubi, she has worked across psychological thrillers, true crime, family drama, romance, and holiday films, always finding the emotional fault line in the material and building outward from there.

That instinct comes from spending more than two decades on the other side of the camera. Hartley logged over 2,000 episodes of network television as an actress, most notably a nine-year run as Theresa Lopez-Fitzgerald on NBC's Passions (1999–2008), which earned her a Soap Opera Digest Award nomination. Her credits also include Days of Our Lives, All My Children, General Hospital, and a turn as DC Comics character Mad Harriet on Smallville.

Her path to directing ran through writing. She has penned more than ten produced screenplays, beginning with Deadly Exchange in 2017, in which she also starred. By the time her tenth screenplay was greenlit, stepping behind the camera was a natural next move. She made her directorial debut with Forgotten Abduction for Lifetime in 2019 and has not stopped since.

In the years since, she has delivered feature after feature on budget and on schedule, building a track record that networks and producers rely on. Her credits include the true-crime feature The Romeo and Juliet Killers for Tubi, the Hulu holiday films Christmas on Repeat and A Christmas Frequency, and a deep run of character-driven thrillers including Dressed to Kill, Tempted, Secrets in the Sisterhood, The Lies We Bury, and Don't Breathe a Word. She is currently producing, directing, and reprising her on-camera role in Paranormal Nobodies, a supernatural coming-of-age series.
